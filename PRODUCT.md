# InstaHelp Product Context

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Node.js backend on Render for forms, plans checkout, and admin. Frontend served from repo root and `backend/public/`.

## What InstaHelp is
InstaHelp is a Bengaluru-based on-demand assistant service operating since April 2015. Customers describe what they need; InstaHelp finds, checks, and coordinates independent providers through to completion. InstaHelp does not perform the underlying work.

## Primary user
Residents and businesses across India who want one team to handle sourcing and coordination instead of calling multiple directory listings.

## Core job
Submit a task (form, WhatsApp, or phone) and have InstaHelp match, coordinate, and follow through with vetted independent providers.

## Differentiation
One coordinating team instead of a list of phone numbers. Provider vetting, pan-India request acceptance, 24/7 availability.
