How we handle complaints

We take every grievance seriously. Here is what happens after you reach out.

  1. 1

    You lodge a complaint

    Submit the form below, email us, or message on WhatsApp with your name, contact details, and a clear description of the issue.

  2. 2

    We acknowledge receipt

    Our team confirms your complaint within 24 hours and assigns a reference number for tracking.

  3. 3

    We investigate

    We review your case, speak with relevant teams or providers where needed, and gather the facts.

  4. 4

    We respond in writing

    You receive a written outcome. Most complaints are resolved within 15 days of acknowledgement.
