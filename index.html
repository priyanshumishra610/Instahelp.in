FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about how InstaHelp works, pricing, coverage, and changes to a booking. Tap any question to expand.

What is InstaHelp?

InstaHelp is a Bengaluru-based assistant service operating since April 2015. You tell us what you need; we find, check and manage the right independent provider. We do not perform the underlying work ourselves. Providers operate on their own account.

How is InstaHelp different from JustDial or IndiaMART?

JustDial and IndiaMART return lists of providers. InstaHelp gives you one team that finds, checks and coordinates a provider through to completion. Instead of a list of numbers, one person who sees it through.

How does InstaHelp work?

Reach us on WhatsApp, phone, or the form above. Share your task, where you are, and when you need it. We match providers we have checked ourselves (see how we vet), clarify scope and pricing where possible, and coordinate until the job is done.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Use the WhatsApp topic shortcuts in the footer for Cancellation or Reschedule, or message us with the phone number or reference used at booking. We align with each provider's policy and confirm changes in writing. Same-day moves depend on partner availability, but we always try for the fastest option.

Is InstaHelp available in my city?

We accept requests from anywhere in India, with our deepest bench in Bengaluru and the major metros. Send your pin code or neighbourhood on WhatsApp and we will confirm coverage before you commit to anything.

How do payments work?

Payment depends on the service. Some third-party providers collect on site, others invoice or share a secure link through us. We explain what you are paying for, including any applicable facilitation fees, before work starts. For billing questions, use the Payment Issues shortcut in the footer so the right desk picks it up quickly.

About InstaHelp

InstaHelp® is a Bengaluru-based assistant service. You tell us what you need; we find, check and manage the right independent provider, and stay on it until it is done. We do not carry out the work ourselves. We source providers nationwide, with our deepest bench in Bengaluru and the major metros.

We take requests from residents of gated communities across Bengaluru localities including Whitefield, Marathahalli, and Sarjapur Road. Send your pin code on WhatsApp and we will confirm coverage and timing before anything is booked.

Data retention

We collect your name, contact details and, only if you choose to share it, your location, in order to match your request to a provider. We keep this for 12 months after your request is closed and then delete it. You can ask us to correct or delete your data at any time at mail@instahelp.in. See our full Privacy Policy for details.
