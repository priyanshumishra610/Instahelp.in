Pricing, Fees & Payment Policy

This Policy governs pricing, platform fees, service charges, consultation fees, coordination fees, commercial facilitation fees, third-party provider payments, and payment arrangements associated with the InstaHelp Platform.

IMPORTANT NOTICE

InstaHelp primarily operates as a technology-enabled commercial intermediation, customer acquisition, communication facilitation, lead-generation, concierge, advertising, marketplace, coordination, and business discovery platform.

Pricing displayed, communicated, estimated, quoted, advertised, or discussed through the Platform may vary significantly depending on the specific request and operational requirements.

1. Pricing May Vary

Pricing for services, consultations, referrals, bookings, introductions, assistance, coordination, concierge requests, and related activities may vary substantially.

Pricing may range from INR 0 to INR 10,000 or more depending on the nature of the request, provider, location, urgency, complexity, availability, and commercial arrangements.

Certain services may be offered free of charge, while others may attract platform fees, consultation fees, provider fees, coordination charges, administrative charges, or commercial facilitation charges.

2. Factors Affecting Pricing

Pricing may depend upon factors including:

  • service category;
  • provider availability;
  • customer requirements;
  • location and travel requirements;
  • urgency and response timelines;
  • duration of engagement;
  • complexity of coordination;
  • number of providers contacted;
  • administrative workload;
  • communication effort;
  • support requirements;
  • market conditions;
  • commercial arrangements;
  • taxes and statutory charges;
  • and other operational considerations.

3. Platform Fees

InstaHelp may charge fees relating to:

  • platform access;
  • consultation services;
  • communication handling;
  • appointment coordination;
  • provider discovery;
  • lead generation;
  • customer support;
  • administrative processing;
  • concierge assistance;
  • business facilitation;
  • commercial intermediation;
  • and operational overheads.

Such fees may be charged independently of any fees payable to third-party providers.

4. Independent Third-Party Provider Charges

Many services available through the Platform are provided by independent third-party businesses, professionals, merchants, agencies, contractors, freelancers, vendors, or service partners.

Pricing for such services is determined by the respective provider and may vary independently of any platform fees charged by InstaHelp.

InstaHelp does not generally control, dictate, approve, or guarantee pricing established by independent third-party providers.

5. Payment Methods

Depending on the service category, provider preference, operational feasibility, or commercial arrangements, payments may be:

  • paid directly to the service provider;
  • paid directly to InstaHelp;
  • collected by InstaHelp on behalf of a provider;
  • split between InstaHelp and the provider;
  • processed through third-party payment gateways;
  • processed through external payment links;
  • or collected through other lawful payment arrangements.

6. Estimates & Quotations

Any estimate, indicative pricing, verbal quote, budgetary figure, approximation, range, or preliminary quotation communicated through the Platform is intended solely as an estimate unless expressly confirmed in writing.

Actual pricing may differ due to changes in scope, provider availability, customer requirements, taxes, market conditions, travel requirements, or operational circumstances.

7. Promotional & Introductory Pricing

InstaHelp may occasionally provide:

  • free consultations;
  • introductory pricing;
  • discounted pricing;
  • promotional credits;
  • subsidized services;
  • referral incentives;
  • campaign offers;
  • or limited-time discounts.
Promotional pricing is discretionary, non-transferable, subject to eligibility requirements, and may be modified or withdrawn at any time without notice.

8. Taxes & Government Charges

Unless expressly stated otherwise, quoted pricing may exclude taxes, duties, levies, payment gateway fees, banking charges, government charges, or statutory costs.

Users remain responsible for all applicable taxes and governmental charges arising from their transactions.

9. Pricing Accuracy

While reasonable efforts are made to maintain accurate information, InstaHelp does not guarantee that all displayed, communicated, estimated, advertised, or published pricing is complete, current, accurate, or continuously available.

Pricing may change without notice due to provider updates, operational requirements, market conditions, technical issues, or commercial considerations.

10. Final Pricing Authority

Final pricing for any service, booking, consultation, referral, introduction, provider engagement, or commercial transaction shall be determined based on the specific circumstances applicable at the time of confirmation.

By using the Platform, users acknowledge and agree that pricing may vary substantially between services, providers, users, locations, and transactions.
