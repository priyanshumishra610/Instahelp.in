Refund & Cancellation Policy

This Policy governs refunds, cancellations, platform fees, consultation charges, communication fees, commercial facilitation charges, and related transactions associated with the InstaHelp Platform.

IMPORTANT NOTICE

InstaHelp primarily operates as a technology-enabled commercial intermediation, advertising, business facilitation, communication coordination, customer acquisition, and marketplace platform.

Fees charged by InstaHelp may relate to:

  • platform access,
  • commercial facilitation,
  • lead generation,
  • customer support,
  • communication handling,
  • administrative processing,
  • appointment coordination,
  • consultation and inquiry handling,
  • business discovery assistance,
  • or operational overheads.

1. General Refund Position

Unless expressly stated otherwise in writing, payments made to InstaHelp are generally:

  • non-refundable,
  • partially refundable,
  • or subject to administrative retention deductions.

This is because substantial operational, communication, coordination, administrative, technology, support, and commercial facilitation activities may commence immediately after a request, inquiry, booking, or payment is initiated.

2. Platform Conversation & Consultation Fees

InstaHelp reserves the right to charge platform conversation, consultation, coordination, inquiry handling, commercial facilitation, or administrative service fees of up to INR 2,500 per conversation, inquiry, request, support interaction, or coordination engagement.

Promotional Discounts

Certain users may receive temporary, introductory, promotional, subsidized, discounted, waived, or reduced pricing for their initial conversations, consultations, or interactions on the Platform.

Such discounts are discretionary, revocable, non-transferable, and may be modified or withdrawn at any time.

3. Cancellation & Retention Rights

If a user:

  • cancels a request,
  • abandons a conversation,
  • fails to proceed,
  • requests reversal after operational work has commenced,
  • or disputes charges after engagement has started,

InstaHelp reserves the right to retain, deduct, or recover applicable platform, consultation, coordination, communication, operational, administrative, and commercial facilitation charges.

The Platform may retain charges of up to INR 2,500 per conversation, request, inquiry, or interaction, even where discounted pricing, promotional credits, subsidies, offers, or reduced introductory pricing were initially provided.

4. Third-Party Services

InstaHelp is generally not the direct provider of most third-party goods or services listed, promoted, advertised, or discussed on the Platform.

Refunds relating to independent third-party providers may depend on:

  • provider policies,
  • merchant terms,
  • service conditions,
  • operational feasibility,
  • or applicable laws.

InstaHelp does not guarantee refunds for third-party transactions, services, bookings, or provider disputes.

5. Processing Timelines

Where refunds are approved, Refund will be processed and "credited" within 28 working day for our fees but it may vary depending on:

  • payment gateway timelines,
  • banking systems,
  • verification requirements,
  • fraud checks,
  • provider coordination,
  • or operational reviews.

6. Fraud, Abuse & Chargebacks

InstaHelp reserves the right to:

  • deny refunds,
  • suspend accounts,
  • restrict access,
  • contest chargebacks,
  • retain records,
  • or initiate recovery actions

where fraud, misuse, abuse, repeated cancellations, suspicious activity, payment disputes, or bad-faith conduct is suspected.

7. Final Interpretation

To the maximum extent permitted by law, InstaHelp reserves reasonable discretion regarding:

  • refund eligibility,
  • administrative deductions,
  • commercial facilitation charges,
  • operational recovery costs,
  • platform fee retention,
  • and dispute handling procedures.
