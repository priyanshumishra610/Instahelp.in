What is InstaHelp?

InstaHelp has been India’s trusted personal Assistant-on-demand since 2015, helping businesses and entrepreneurs grow, streamline operations, and unlock new revenue opportunities.

We act as a powerful Assistant-led intermediation platform, connecting your business to high-intent customers across home services, office functions, business support tasks, travel, and more.

How does InstaHelp work?

Reach us on WhatsApp, phone, App or the detailed form above. Share your task, where you are, and when you need it. We match vetted partners, clarify scope and pricing where possible, and coordinate until the job is done. You keep a single point of contact instead of chasing multiple vendors.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Use the WhatsApp topic shortcuts in the footer for Cancellation or Reschedule, or message us with the phone number or reference used at booking. We align with each provider's policy and confirm changes in writing. Same-day moves depend on partner availability, but we always try for the fastest option.

Is InstaHelp available in my city?

We serve customers across India, including Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore and many other towns and metros. If you are unsure, send your pin code or neighbourhood on WhatsApp and we will confirm coverage before you commit to anything.

How do payments work?

Payment depends on the service. Some third-party providers collect on site, others invoice or share a secure link through us. We explain what you are paying for, including any applicable facilitation fees, before work starts. For billing questions, use the Payment Issues shortcut in the footer so the right desk picks it up quickly.

About InstaHelp

InstaHelp® operates as a Assistant-based commercial intermediation platform (Trademark Class 35) connecting users with verified third-party service providers across India. Whether you are looking for InstaHelp home services (repairs, cleaning, maintenance), InstaHelp business support (CAs, legal, procurement, office functions), or InstaHelp travel and event coordination, our human Assistant team handles end-to-end sourcing, arranging contracts and coordinating delivery on your behalf.

We arrange and intermediate trusted professionals with deep operational coverage across all major regional hubs in Tier-1 metros like Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, and Kolkata. We also bring the same seamless coordination to booming Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities (coming soon), including Pune, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Surat, Siliguri, Coimbatore, Amritsar, Lucknow, Indore, Bhopal, Chandigarh, Kochi and many more.

Instead of endlessly searching for "reliable services near me," simply tell us your task. InstaHelp® will intermediate the right local expert matched to your exact location and timeline. We do not perform the underlying work directly - we arrange it through verified providers under our Class 35 mandate of commercial intermediation, business management for others, and arranging contracts for the supply of services.
